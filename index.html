How the numbers are calculated

  • Returns use daily closing prices in US dollars. Volatility is the standard deviation of daily log returns, scaled by √365 because crypto trades every day.
  • Max drawdown is the largest fall from a previous high to a later low within the period.
  • Beta and correlation compare each coin's daily returns with Bitcoin's on the same days. A beta of 1.5 means the coin has typically moved 1.5% for each 1% move in Bitcoin.
  • Stablecoins are coins whose price barely moves (annualised volatility under 5% over 90 days). Wrapped tokens such as WBTC or stETH track another coin's price. Both are left out of the risk charts.
  • Data: prices, market values and rankings come live from CoinPaprika; daily price history from the public market data of Kraken, with Coinbase and Binance.US as fallbacks. A scheduled job saves a year of history for the top 30 each day, which the page uses for the risk charts and whenever the live feeds are unavailable.
